From the principal's desk

A school where every student is known by name.

Plus One and Plus Two are short years. We use them to build steady study habits, and we stay in touch with every family while we do it.

Since 1978 the school has prepared students from Kottayam and the surrounding panchayats for the state higher secondary examinations. Class teachers meet parents each term, the labs stay open after hours before practical exams, and the library keeps past question papers for every subject.
