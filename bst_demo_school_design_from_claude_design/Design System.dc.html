BST Higher Secondary · design system

Crisp, bright and Kerala-coloured.

Roboto, brand red #B1040E for every action, 4px buttons, 8px cards and a seven-colour palette that gives each stream, event and result its own colour. Nothing here depends on the earlier Modernist system. Pages: Home, directions A and B. Everything is defined in school-ds.css, which maps one-to-one onto the Tailwind theme for handoff.

Color

Red is the only action colour. Every other hue has a job: it marks a stream, a campus area, an event type or a result, and always comes as a strong tone plus a soft 50 tint with a dark text tone that passes AA.

The two directions

A · corporate. White and mist grounds, ink stats band, red CTA. Colour arrives through the streams and accents.

B · warm Kerala. Cream grounds, deep green bands and footer, gold highlights. Red stays on every action.

Body text note. The brief's 60% ink measures about 4.4:1 on white and less on mist, which fails AA. Body text uses 76% (7.5:1); small captions use 68% (5.3:1).

Layout and shape

Content width

1280px max, gutters 20px on mobile up to 64px on desktop.

Spacing

8px grid: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40. Sections 56px to 104px top and bottom.

Radius

Buttons and fields 4px, cards and panels 8px, avatars and step numbers round.

Depth

Hairline border plus a very soft shadow on cards. Hover lifts 3px and deepens the shadow.

Alternating grounds

White and mist sections alternate; a dark band and a full-width image band break the rhythm.

Breakpoints

Mobile under 640px, tablet 640–1079px, desktop 1080px up. Layouts respond to the container width.

Buttons and tags

4px radius, 600 weight, 44px minimum height. Red fill is the primary action everywhere; outline is secondary; text buttons carry quiet links.

Motion and accessibility

Motion

Scroll reveal: an 18px rise and fade, CSS only, never fully transparent. Hover: color, border and a 3px lift, 180ms. Stats count up once over 1.4s. With prefers-reduced-motion, reveals, lifts and the count-up are off.

Accessibility

Text 4.5:1 or better; every palette colour used for small text has a dark text tone. Colour never carries meaning alone: stream, category and status also appear as words. Focus is a 3px blue outline, white on dark bands. Hit targets are 44px or taller.

Semantics

One h1, sections with headings, real buttons and links, labelled inputs, alt text on every image slot, lang="ml" on Malayalam text.
